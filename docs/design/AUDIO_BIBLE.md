# Audio & Music Bible

## Identity
Minimal electronic office ambience mixed with dry acoustic details. The soundtrack becomes denser as the company scales.

## Layers
- Room tone: HVAC, distant typing, soft server fan texture.
- Interaction: restrained tactile clicks, paper, chair, keyboard, rack latch.
- Alerts: short tonal motifs, never mobile-OS clones.
- Music: original generative/procedural stems, 80–105 BPM, minor/ambiguous harmony, no imitation of existing game themes.

## Dynamic music states
1. Garage: sparse synth plucks + room tone.
2. Growth: bass pulse + light percussion.
3. Launch: subtle ostinato.
4. Incident: filtered pulse/tension layer.
5. Late game: wider pads, machine rhythm.

## Loudness rules
UI clicks quiet; alerts distinct without startling. Master/Music/SFX/Ambience/Voice buses separately adjustable.

## Included placeholders
`game/assets/audio/` includes original procedural WAVs generated by `tools/generate_audio.py`. They are placeholders, not a final mastered soundtrack.
