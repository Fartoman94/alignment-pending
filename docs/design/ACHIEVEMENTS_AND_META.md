# Achievements & replay meta

Achievements should reward interesting play, not grind or real-company references.

Draft examples:
- **Hello, World?** Ship the first public model.
- **Known Unknowns** Release with three unresolved evaluation warnings.
- **Read the Memo** Delay a launch after a safety recommendation.
- **Five Nines-ish** Reach high reliability for 30 in-game days.
- **The Runway Is a Number** Survive with under seven days of cash runway.
- **Capacity Planning** Avoid any compute saturation for one full act.
- **Rollback Friday** Successfully roll back a troubled release.
- **Everybody Gets a Dashboard** Employ 50 staff.
- **Not a Monopoly** Finish with under 50% market share and positive cash flow.
- **Alignment Pending** Reach the final act.

Avoid achievements requiring harmful/illegal real-world analogies. Final list after playtesting so achievements track actual fun behaviors.
