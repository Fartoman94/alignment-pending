# Systems catalog

## S01 Time & simulation
Pausable clock, deterministic seeded RNG, queued daily/weekly ticks.

## S02 Building
Grid-snapped placement, rooms, object requirements, navigation validation, refunds.

## S03 Staff
Roles, skills, salary, morale, fatigue, values, relationships, tasks, resignations.

## S04 Compute
GPU-equivalent abstract units, rack capacity, heat, power, training/inference allocation.

## S05 Research
Tech tree with capability, efficiency, safety, interpretability, infrastructure and organization branches.

## S06 Model training
Configure project, allocate data/compute/staff, simulate progress, produce model artifact.

## S07 Evaluation
Benchmark suites reveal estimates with uncertainty. Safety eval depth reduces unknowns but costs time.

## S08 Release management
Internal/Beta/Public/Open release modes, staged rollout, rate limits, rollback.

## S09 Users & revenue
Segments: hobbyist, developer, business, education, government. Different demand/cost/risk.

## S10 Incidents
Rule-based event engine with trigger context, severity, discovery delay, response choices, aftermath.

## S11 Trust/media
Public trust and audience-specific sentiment. PR can communicate but cannot erase evidence.

## S12 Regulation/legal
Jurisdictions, audits, disclosure requirements, fines, injunction risk, lobbying/legal staffing.

## S13 Competitors
Simulated rivals with budgets, research pace, releases and strategic reactions.

## S14 Board/investors
Funding rounds, valuation, board demands, control tradeoffs.

## S15 Agents/autonomy
Automation unlocks productivity and new failure modes.

## S16 World state
Energy prices, chip supply, regulation climate, labor market, public mood.

## S17 Narrative/endings
Milestone events and epilogue evaluator.

## S18 Save/load
Versioned JSON/binary-safe model with migrations and autosave rotation.
