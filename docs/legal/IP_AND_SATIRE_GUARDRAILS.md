# IP, brand and satire guardrails

This is production guidance, not legal advice.

## Naming
**ALIGNMENT PENDING is a working title.** Before public announcement/Steam page, perform professional trademark clearance in relevant territories and storefront-title checks.

## Fiction boundary
Do not use real AI companies, model names, executive likenesses, logos, slogans, product UI screenshots, voice clones, or confusingly similar brand designs.

## Satire approach
Satirize industry incentives using composite fictional systems. Avoid statements that a real identifiable entity committed crimes, deception, theft, safety violations or other misconduct.

## Assets
No “found on Google” assets. No stock unless explicitly licensed and logged. No texture ripping. No music imitation. No trademarked iconography.

## Fonts
Use Godot/system fallback during development. Final fonts must be OFL/public-domain/commercially licensed and recorded.

## AI-assisted development
The game credits do not need to be written as a joke naming development assistants. However, storefront/platform disclosures must follow the rules in force at submission time. Do not conceal required disclosures.

## Final legal checkpoint
Before marketing lock:
- title/trademark clearance,
- contractor IP assignments,
- asset license audit,
- privacy/telemetry review,
- platform content survey,
- EULA/privacy policy if needed.
